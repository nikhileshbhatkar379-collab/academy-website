About Us

Nikhillesh Bhatkar Learning Academy is a premier coaching institute located in Sangameshwar (near Ninavi Temple), dedicated to academic excellence for Maharashtra State Board students. We provide focused, concept-driven coaching for Standards 8, 9, 10, and 12, along with innovative skill development programs in Financial Markets and Artificial Intelligence.
